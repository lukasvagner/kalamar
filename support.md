# Kalamar support

[Česky](support.cs.md)

Something not working, an idea, or a question? Write to **kalamar.dev@gmail.com**. Say which iPad and
iPadOS version you have and what you did just before; a screenshot or a short screen recording helps
a lot.

## Common questions
**Where are my notes?** On your iPad, in the app. You can also see them in the Files app under
On My iPad › Kalamar.

**How do I back them up?** Settings → Backup → choose a folder (on the iPad or in iCloud Drive,
Google Drive, OneDrive…). Kalamar then copies what changed there, with older versions. Restore
brings notebooks back from that folder, for example on a new iPad.

**How do I move my notes from another app?** New → Import from Noteful brings Noteful notebooks in
with the ink still editable. From any other app, export a PDF and open it in Kalamar (or share it to
Kalamar): you can write on it.

**Which gestures are there?** Settings → Help shows them with small animations, and
Kalamar shows a tip the first time something could be done faster.

**Calculate read my writing wrong.** Settings → Handwriting → Teach my handwriting: write the
digits and letters once the way you write them. When a reading is wrong, correct it: Kalamar learns
from corrections.

**Does Kalamar need the internet?** No. It works fully offline. Only things you choose (Ask AI,
links, a cloud backup folder) use the internet, through other apps.

**Which iPads?** iPads with iPadOS 17 or later. The Apple Pencil is recommended; finger drawing can
be turned on.
