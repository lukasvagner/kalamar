# Kalamar privacy policy

*Last updated: 10 November 2026* · [Česky](privacy.cs.md)

Kalamar is a note-taking app for the iPad, made by Lukas Vagner ("we" below). **It collects nothing
about you.** There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking and no servers of ours:
the app sends nothing to us or to anyone else.

## Where your notes are
- **On your iPad.** Notebooks, recordings, stickers, flash card decks, your handwriting samples and
  settings are kept in the app's storage on the iPad (the notebooks are visible in the Files app,
  On My iPad › Kalamar). Deleting the app deletes them, but not your backups.
- **In the backup folder you choose.** If you turn on automatic backup (Settings → Backup), Kalamar
  copies your notebooks into the folder you picked in the Files app. If that folder is in a cloud
  drive (iCloud Drive, Google Drive, OneDrive…), that service stores the copies under its own
  privacy policy. Kalamar never picks a folder by itself.
- **On your own PC, if you use the viewer.** The Kalamar viewer on your PC receives your notes
  directly over your local network, after you enter its address and pairing code. Nothing goes
  through the internet or through us.

## What the app uses, and when
- **Handwriting reading** (search, To text, Calculate, Graph, circuits) runs on the iPad with
  Apple's on-device frameworks. Your writing isn't sent anywhere. The samples you write in
  Settings → Handwriting stay on the iPad.
- **The microphone** is used only while you record (the microphone button). Recordings stay in the
  notebook.
- **The camera** is used only when you take a photo or scan a document into a notebook.
- **Photos:** you pick pictures to insert with the system picker, which gives the app only the
  pictures you choose. Kalamar saves to your photo library only when you choose Save Image.
- **The local network** is used only for the PC viewer, when you turn on Live view on PC.
- **Ask AI and Export for AI** copy a picture of the screen or the notes you choose, and open the
  AI app or website you picked (for example Claude, ChatGPT or Gemini), or the share sheet. What you
  then paste or send there is between you and that service, under its own terms and privacy
  policy. Kalamar itself sends nothing.
- **Links** in your notes open in Safari or another app only when you choose to open them.
- **Imports** (PDFs, Noteful and GoodNotes notebooks, pictures) are read from the files you pick and kept on the
  iPad.

## Your rights
We hold no personal data about you, so there is nothing we can show, correct, hand over or delete.
Your notes are on your devices and in the backup folder you chose: you can open, export or delete
them at any time in the app or in the Files app. If you live in the EU or the UK you have the
rights the GDPR gives you; since we process no personal data, they don't need any action from us,
but you can always contact us (below) or your data protection authority (in the Czech Republic,
the Úřad pro ochranu osobních údajů, www.uoou.cz).

## Children
Kalamar is suitable for all ages and collects no data from anyone, children included.

## Changes
If this policy changes, the new version is posted here with a new date. If the app ever starts
collecting any data, this policy will say so before the version of the app that does it is released.
